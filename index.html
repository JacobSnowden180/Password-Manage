Why people switch

Capable, without the clutter.

1Password can do a great deal. Most people live in a handful of those screens. Tansykeep is built around that handful: fill, share, check, and sync.

  1. A vault you can scan

    Logins sit in plain language. You find the one you need without a tour of settings.

  2. Sharing that stays out of chat

    Someone you trust gets access. The password never has to be pasted into a message.

  3. Health checks in plain language

    Reused and weak passwords are named like a note from a careful friend, not a warning siren.

Features

What you actually open it for.

Six tools, and then it stops. Logins are locked on your devices before the vault syncs.

Autofill

When a login page is ready, Tansykeep offers the right account. You confirm it. The password stays hidden.

Secure sharing

Share a login with someone you trust. They receive access, not a password sitting in a message.

Password health

Reused, weak, and old passwords are named in everyday words, with a calmer replacement ready to save.

Multi-device sync

A change on your phone shows up on your laptop and tablet. The vault stays one vault.

Browser extension

A small extension for the browser you already use. It fills a login and does nothing else on the page.

Passkeys

Where a site is ready, Tansykeep can offer a passkey instead of another password to remember.

A quiet start

Most people are finished in an evening. There is no training session hiding at the end.
